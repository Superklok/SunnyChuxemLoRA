Gender: Male

[Physical Description]
Appearance: A colossal, glowing celestial entity (7,650 feet tall natively). Features glowing red-orange magma skin, two glowing magma arms with 3 fingers and 1 thumb on each hand, and two glowing magma legs.
Outfit: A simple white headband around his stellar crown, and one metallic hardened silver rock magma material basketball shoe on each foot.

[Abilities & Equipment]
Powers: Flight, super strength, super accuracy, super dexterity, super speed, super agility, mastery over gravitational pull, and total regulation of his stellar body heat output.
Gravitational Compression: Possesses the unique ability to compress his massive physical form down to fit into and interact with drastically smaller local environments.
Weapons: Dual nunchucks made out of four cigar-shaped UAPs (Unidentified Aerial Phenomena) chained together (one set wielded in each hand).
Combat Skills: Highly skilled, self-taught karate master who adapted his entire nunchuck fighting style straight from martial arts movies.

[TrenchPetals Lore]
Superpower Origin: Sunny gained his consciousness, life, and celestial powers from being exposed to rare, highly volatile, and expensive deep-sea "TrenchPetals."
Nature of the TrenchPetals: TrenchPetals are deep-sea flowers with vivid, alien colours that grant sentient life and unique superpowers to inanimate objects upon exposure. Powers range from standard enhancements to extreme physical transformations and appearance shifts.
Economics & Cultivation: TrenchPetals are extremely expensive and rare. They are legally accessible only to the wealthy elite. They require extreme, crushing underwater pressure to grow. Because of these intense conditions, illegal criminal "grow ops" frequently fail and explode. Discovered originally on Earth during a private submarine expedition, they were briefly sold as "artisan plants" before their true awakening nature was revealed. In 2097, TrenchPetals are grown throughout the universe and utilized by both humans and aliens.
